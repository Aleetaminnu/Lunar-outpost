# Sources and fact check

Every fact the game states is listed here with where it comes from.

| What the game says | Source |
|---|---|
| Moon surface radiation about 1.37 mSv per day (1,369 microsieverts); ISS about 0.73 mSv per day over the same period | Zhang et al., "First measurements of the radiation dose on the lunar surface", Science Advances 6 (2020), doi 10.1126/sciadv.aaz1334 (Chang'e 4 lander) |
| Mars surface radiation about 0.67 mSv per day | Hassler et al., Science 343 (2014), Curiosity RAD; Southwest Research Institute press release, Dec 2013 |
| X9.0 flare on 3 Oct 2024, the strongest of Solar Cycle 25 at that time | NASA Solar Cycle 25 blog, 3 Oct 2024 (science.nasa.gov/blogs/solar-cycle-25/2024/10/03/sun-releases-strong-solar-flare-6); NOAA NESDIS |
| G5 geomagnetic storm on 10-11 May 2024, the first G5 since 2003 | NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center, reported by NPR (10 May 2024) |
| Sept 2017 solar storm gave Mars surface radiation more than double any earlier Curiosity reading | NASA JPL news release, 29 Sept 2017 |
| Opportunity fell silent in June 2018 during a global dust storm that blocked its solar power | NASA JPL Opportunity mission page (last contact 10 June 2018) |
| Radio signals take 4 to 24 minutes one way between Earth and Mars; about 1.3 seconds to the Moon | NASA Goddard, "Space communications: 7 things you need to know" (nasa.gov) |
| The Moon's night lasts about 14 Earth days | Widely stated in NASA-linked material on lunar landers and the lunar day; for example Intuitive Machines and NASA briefings on the Odysseus lander (Feb 2024) |
| ISS crews have grown lettuce and chile peppers | NASA, Plant Habitat-04 (chile peppers, 2021) and the Veggie system (lettuce since 2015) |
| Piling moon soil (regolith) over a habitat is one idea for blocking radiation | NASA Technical Reports Server: "Radiation protective structure alternatives for habitats of a lunar base research outpost"; NASA Lunar Safe Haven concept (2021) |
| Space weather level in the game (live) | NASA DONKI solar flare (FLR) data through api.nasa.gov, asked at most once every 30 minutes per browser |

Simplified for gameplay: power amounts, how much each building restores, the daily drop in air, food and shield, and how health is lost.
